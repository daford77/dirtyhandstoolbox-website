iPhone / iPad tip: Save it like an app

After opening the web app in Safari, add it to your Home Screen:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Select Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then launch Dirty Hands Toolbox from your Home Screen.

Android users: download from Google Play for the full native experience. iPhone and iPad users can open the web app in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen.
