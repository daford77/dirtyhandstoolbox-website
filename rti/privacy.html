Privacy Policy for RTI Calculator

Last updated: August 11, 2026

RTI Calculator is published by Dirty Hands Toolbox. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, how that information is handled, and the choices available to users.

Information the app stores

The app may store the following information that you enter or capture while using it:

  • Event details such as event name, date, and description
  • Participant information such as name, license plate, and email address
  • Vehicle details such as make, model, year, class, and setup notes
  • RTI attempt data such as wheelbase, ramp distance, ramp angle, vertical travel, score, and timestamp
  • Optional photos or videos attached to attempts

How information is used

The app uses this information to:

  • Calculate RTI scores and organize event results
  • Create exports such as PDF reports, CSV files, and shareable images
  • Support optional camera-based features such as scanning or capturing media
  • Support optional Pro features, including cloud sync and public leaderboard publishing

Local storage on your device

Data you enter is stored locally on your device so the app can function offline. Export files and shareable images are also created temporarily on your device when you choose to export or share them.

Cloud services and sync

Pro features may sync app data to Google Firebase services, including Firebase Firestore and Firebase Storage. This can include event records, participant information, vehicle information, attempt details, and any optional media you attach to attempts.

Public leaderboard and portal

When Pro sync is used, leaderboard-related information may also be published to the RTI public web portal. That public information can include event details, participant names, vehicle class, RTI scores, and related event results.

Camera access

The app requests camera permission only for features that need it, such as capturing photos or helping scan information. Camera access is optional, but some features will not work without it.

Purchases

If you purchase Pro features, billing is processed by Google Play. RTI Calculator does not store your full payment card information. The app only stores the purchase status needed to unlock Pro features.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as needed to provide app functionality, including Google Play billing, optional Firebase sync, and any sharing or export actions you choose to perform.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. If data has been synced or published through Pro features, it may remain in connected cloud services or the public portal until it is removed there.

Children's privacy

RTI Calculator is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions about RTI Calculator, please use the contact details provided on the Dirty Hands Toolbox website or in the Google Play listing for the app.
